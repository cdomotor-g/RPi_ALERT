RPi ALERT

A Raspberry Pi base station for ALERT flood-warning telemetry. Plug in an RTL-SDR stick, a Quansheng radio on the ALERT firmware or an ELPRO ERT-A2, and every burst it hears goes to MegaNet — decoded by the same code the website runs, reconnected by itself after an unplug or a power cut.

    Put the Pi's microSD card (8 GB or larger) in this computer. A browser cannot write a whole SD card itself — no web page can, for safety — so one of these does the writing:

    4 Start it
    
      
      
        
    • Put the card in the Pi. Plug in the receivers — RTL-SDR stick with its antenna, the Quansheng radio by USB-C (ALERT app running), the ERT-A2 by its USB-serial cable — then the network and the power.
      • 
        
    • Give it two minutes the first time. Then open http://rpi-alert.local/ from a computer on the same network (or the Pi's address from your router).
      • 
        
    • With a monitor plugged in, the same dashboard appears full screen. Over SSH (PuTTY on Windows): ssh <user>@rpi-alert.local, then rpi-alert status or rpi-alert setup.
      • 
        
    • A speaker in the audio jack (Pi 3/4) plays each ALERT burst. The Pi 5 and Zero have no jack: use HDMI audio or a USB sound card.
